## Problem Statement

While working across multiple projects, I noticed a recurring problem: components were being rebuilt again and again with inconsistent styles, behaviors, and naming conventions. This led to bugs, delivery delays, and design drift.  
Karass DS is a personal initiative that synthesizes learnings from those real-world inconsistencies into a cohesive system — documenting reusable components, styling tokens, and scalable APIs using Storybook.
