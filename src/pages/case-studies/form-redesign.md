## Problem Statement

Legal assistants and operations staff relied on a long, unclear Django-generated form to upload contracts in the internal CMS. The form lacked structure, provided no guidance, and often resulted en errores, confusión y tickets de soporte innecesarios.  
The goal was to make this form understandable, faster to complete, and easier to maintain — turning a frustrating experience into a scalable UX pattern for future workflows.
